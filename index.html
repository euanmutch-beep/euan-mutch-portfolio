My Story

From rock bottom to building a movement that changes lives

Lived Experience. Professional Expertise.

I grew up in the care system, navigating instability, addiction, and depression. For years I felt lost, believing there was no way out. Fitness became my turning point — giving me structure, discipline, and hope.

With the support of my wife Tracy, I qualified as a Level 2 Gym Instructor then progressed to Level 3 Personal Trainer. Gaining CIMSPA status and becoming a FitPro Professional Member were milestones I never thought possible. Over 35 years of coaching experience.

Today, through New Mindset New Power, I help others rebuild confidence, break unhelpful habits, and create sustainable change. My approach combines structured training with mindset work, supportive accountability, and genuine lived experience.

Mission: Build strong bodies, strong minds, and strong communities — one person at a time.

“I know what it’s like to feel like you’re at rock bottom. Through fitness, education, and community, I rebuilt myself. Now I dedicate my life to helping others do the same. Because no matter how lost you feel, it’s never too late to change your story.”

— Euan Mutch, Founder, New Mindset New Power
